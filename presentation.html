Care Silos

Clinical Safety

🛡️ AI Drug-Drug Interaction Engine

Real-time pharmacology conflict detection. Automatically warns if prescribed drugs conflict (e.g. Warfarin + Aspirin bleeding danger, Lisinopril + Potassium hyperkalemia).

Emotional Gamification

❤️ Family "Love Nudges" & Cheer Wall

Grandchildren and family send encouraging notes with emojis. When Arthur logs a pill, celebratory confetti explodes and the cheer note pops up!

Emergency Response

🚨 1-Tap Emergency SOS & Medical ID

Sounds an audible siren, alerts caregivers immediately, and displays blood group O+, severe penicillin allergies, and active medications for first responders.

Smart Logistics

📉 Auto Inventory & Refill Predictor

Decrements pill counts on intake. When stock reaches 7 pills, alerts caretaker and generates a 1-click Google Calendar pharmacy refill reminder.
